# Language Quality

Apply language cleanup after factual content is established.

1. Preserve numbers, units, dates, product names, task IDs, evidence IDs, source IDs, and uncertainty exactly unless the source evidence supports a correction.
2. Match the requested language and audience. Prefer natural professional wording over literal translation.
3. In Japanese R&D communication, favor concise factual sentences, explicit status, issue, cause, action, and next-step wording.
4. Do not turn a hypothesis into a fact, a draft into a completion, or an unresolved item into a recommendation.
5. Keep technical terminology consistent across headings, tables, notes, and summaries.
6. If the source text is ambiguous, retain the ambiguity or flag it instead of guessing.
7. This skill is instruction-only and grants no external translation, telemetry, network, or upload authority.
